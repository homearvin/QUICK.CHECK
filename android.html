QuickCheck for Android

QuickCheck on your phone

The same QuickCheck as on your laptop, on an Android phone: check papers with the camera, see the score at once, and keep your classes with you.

Download the phone app

Open this page on your Android phone, then press the button.

Download for Android

Android 7 or newer · about 73 MB · free for 7 days, then the same key as your laptop · updates itself

🔑

One key: your laptop and your phone. A QuickCheck key works on one laptop and one phone. When both have the same key and are online, press Sync now and your classes, tests and scores move between them — no password, no account.

1

Install it

About three minutes. The app is not from the Play Store, so your phone asks a few times.

Your phone will warn you. That is normal. QuickCheck is installed from this website, not from the Play Store, and Android warns about every app installed this way, even safe ones. That is why you see words like “harmful” or “unknown app”. QuickCheck does not read your contacts, messages or location; your classes and scores stay on your phone.
  1. Open this page in Chrome. Got the link in Messenger or Facebook? Their own browser often blocks downloads: tap ⋮ at the top → Open in Chrome (or “Open in browser”).
  2. Press Download for Android above. If Chrome says “File might be harmful” or “This type of file can harm your device”, tap Download anyway. Wait until the download finishes (about 73 MB — Wi-Fi is best).
  3. Tap Open, or tap the downloaded file QuickCheck-Mobile.apk (in the notification, or in Files → Downloads).
  4. If Android says “your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps”, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, and go back.
  5. Tap Install. If Play Protect says “Unsafe app blocked” or “App scan recommended”, tap More details → Install anyway (or Scan app, then Install). That warning appears for any app that is not from the Play Store.
  6. Open QuickCheck. The very first start takes about 20 seconds; after that it opens quickly.
Still blocked? Some phones have one more lock Samsung (newer Galaxy): if nothing happens or it says “blocked by Auto Blocker”, go to Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker → turn it off, install QuickCheck, then turn it back on.
Huawei / Honor: if it says the app is not verified, turn off Pure mode (Settings → System & updates → Pure mode), or tap Continue installation.
Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO, Oppo, Vivo, Realme: after the security scan, tap Install anyway or Continue; some ask for your phone's password first.
Menu names can differ a little by phone model and Android version — message us and we'll help.
2

Enter your key

Use the same key file as your laptop. It needs the internet this once.

  1. Put your key file (it ends in .qckey) on the phone — the easiest way is to send it to yourself on Messenger or Gmail, then download it on the phone.
  2. In QuickCheck, tap Buy or enter a key in the yellow bar and choose your .qckey file.
One phone per keyChanging phones? Message us and we move your key to the new phone, free — the same as moving to a new laptop.
3

Check papers with the camera

One paper at a time: photo, score, next.

  1. Open a test: Tests → the test → Check → Take a paper.
  2. Hold the phone flat above the sheet with all four black corner squares in view, and take the photo.
  3. The checked paper appears at once: the learner's name, the score, green for correct and red for wrong. An unclear mark is circled in amber — tap the right letter.
  4. Tap Save & next paper. It saves the score and opens the camera for the next paper.
4

Sync with your laptop

Both with the same key, both online.

  1. On the laptop: Settings → Sync → Sync now.
  2. On the phone: Settings → Sync → Sync now. The Sync page lists what was sent and received.
  3. It also syncs by itself every few minutes while QuickCheck is open and online.
What travelsClasses, tests and answer keys, scores, and your name and school — both ways. Your laptop's class record (Excel or QuickCheck's own) stays the official copy: scores checked on the phone wait on the laptop until you press Put them in. Photos of the papers stay on the phone.
Needs QuickCheck 2.11 or newer on the laptopOlder laptop versions don't have Sync yet; the phone app works on its own until the laptop is updated (QuickCheck offers the update by itself).
5

Updates

Nothing to download by hand again.

  1. When a new version is out, QuickCheck says so when it opens. Tap Update, then Android's Update. Your classes, tests and scores stay.
  2. To check any time: Settings → About → Check for updates.
