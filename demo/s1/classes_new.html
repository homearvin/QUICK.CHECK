Terms & columns DepEd
Weights follow DepEd Order No. 015, s. 2026. Examinations are split the DepEd way — ST1 30%, ST2 30% and the term exam 40% of that share — so for core subjects the 30% is recorded as Summative Test 18% (2 columns) and Term Examination 12% (1 column). The percentages are recorded and shown, never used to compute a grade.
