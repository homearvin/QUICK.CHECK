Notes on Computation

  • Mean Percentage Score (MPS) = (mean score ÷ highest possible score) × 100, reported to two decimal places. The highest possible score is the number of items when every item is worth 1 point.
  • Proficiency levels (DepEd National Achievement Test criteria): 90–100 Highly Proficient; 75–89 Proficient; 50–74 Nearly Proficient; 25–49 Low Proficient; 0–24 Not Proficient. A percentage is placed by the lower bound of its range, so 74.50 is Nearly Proficient.
  • Standard deviation (SD) is computed over all learners tested (population SD).
  • This report records results only; it does not compute or replace grades.
